# 19 — Risk register

Conventions: [`README.md`](README.md). Likelihood and impact are rated **H** (high), **M** (medium) or **L** (low).
Impact means loss of data, functions, users or links, or a production incident. Review this register at every
milestone exit (13) and before each release stage (18).
